Groovy Android Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

Groovy is developed and provided by Yvente. This Privacy Policy applies to the Android version of Groovy (the “App”). It explains what information the App processes, where it is stored, and when information is transmitted from your device.

1. Information the App Processes

1.1 Audio Files and Embedded Metadata

When you explicitly import audio files using the Android file picker, the App processes those files to provide playback and library management. The App may read information embedded in an imported audio file, such as its title, artist, album, track number, duration, bit rate, sample rate, and embedded cover artwork.

  • Imported audio files are copied into the App’s private storage on your device.
  • Audio files and their metadata are processed locally on your device.
  • The App does not upload audio files to the developer or to a Groovy server.
  • The Android v1 App does not provide online cover-art matching or online lyrics matching.

1.2 App Settings and Playback Information

The App stores information needed to provide its features, including your music library, playlists, favorites, playback state, play count, last-played time, appearance settings, and other app preferences. This information remains in the App’s private storage on your device.

1.3 App Review Prompt

The App may request an optional in-app review through Google Play after you have used the App for a period of time. The review prompt is provided by Google Play. The App does not operate an analytics or advertising service and does not use the review prompt to build a user profile.

2. How Information Is Used

The App uses locally processed information only to:

  • Play and organize the audio files that you import
  • Display audio metadata and embedded artwork
  • Manage songs, albums, playlists, favorites, playback counts, and last-played times
  • Apply your playback and appearance preferences
  • Provide an optional Google Play in-app review prompt

3. Information Sharing and External Services

The App does not sell, rent, or share your audio files, music library, playlists, or personal information with the developer or other companies. The Android v1 App has no developer-operated cloud account, online music-library sync, online cover-art matching, online lyrics matching, advertising, or analytics service.

When the optional in-app review prompt is requested, Google Play may process information under Google’s own terms and privacy policy. The App does not control Google Play’s processing.

4. Storage and Retention

  • Audio files are stored in the App’s private, device-local storage.
  • Music library records and preferences are stored locally using Android device storage mechanisms.
  • The developer does not retain a copy of your imported files or library data on its servers.
  • Deleting a song or clearing the library removes the corresponding local records and, where applicable, the copied audio files from the App.
  • Uninstalling the App generally removes its private local data, subject to Android system behavior and any files you separately retain or export.

5. Your Choices and Controls

You can:

  • Choose which files or folders to import
  • Delete songs, playlists, and local library data from within the App
  • Uninstall the App to remove its private local data
  • Contact us with privacy-related questions

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not provide an online account or developer-operated cloud service, it is not designed to collect children’s personal information.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last Updated” date.
