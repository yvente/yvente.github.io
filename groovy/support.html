Groovy Support

Last Updated: October 28, 2026

Groovy is a local music player for iPhone and iPad. It imports audio files from your device or over Wi-Fi, manages your library with playlists, albums, and folders, and plays them with a vinyl-inspired interface. This page answers common questions and explains how to reach us.

Contact Us

For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, email us at yvente@163.com.

To help us investigate quickly, please include:

  • Your device model (for example, iPhone 17 Pro) and iOS or iPadOS version
  • The App version, shown at the bottom of the Settings screen
  • The steps that led to the problem and the exact message you saw

Please do not attach your audio files or personal data unless we ask for them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What audio formats are supported?

MP3, FLAC, WAV, AAC, M4A, M4B, MP4, ALAC, AIFF, AIFC, CAF, and AU. Imported files are stored inside the App's own folder on your device.

How do I add music?

Open the Import screen and choose files from the Files app. You can also import an entire folder; its subfolder structure is preserved and can be browsed in the folder view of the Songs tab. On first use, iOS may ask for permission to access the files you select.

How does Wi-Fi transfer work?

Wi-Fi transfer is a Pro feature. Start it on the Import screen, then open the address shown there in a web browser on a computer connected to the same Wi-Fi network and upload your audio files. The transfer runs locally between your devices, and the server stops when you leave the transfer screen.

What are the limits of the free version?

The free version holds up to 50 songs and 3 playlists. Pro removes both limits.

What does Pro include?

  • Unlimited songs and playlists
  • The complete vinyl theme collection
  • 10-band equalizer with built-in presets
  • Vinyl crackle sound effect
  • Wi-Fi transfer
  • Online cover art matching and lyrics matching

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. To restore it on a new device, open Settings, select Unlock Groovy Pro, and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Restoring never charges you again.

How do Play Next and the queue work?

Long-press a song and choose Play Next to place it right after the current track, or Add to Queue to append it. Open the queue from the player to see upcoming songs, play any row, or remove items with a swipe. In shuffle mode, the queue shows the actual playback order.

What playback modes are available?

Sequential, repeat all, repeat one, and shuffle. Switch modes from the player, Driving Mode, or the system controls. Skipping manually still moves to the next or previous track in repeat-one mode; only finishing a track repeats it.

Does playback continue in the background?

Yes. Groovy keeps playing when you leave the App or lock the screen. You can control it from the Lock Screen and Control Center, the AirPlay menu, the Home Screen widgets, and the Control Center controls for Play/Pause and Play Favorites. The current track also appears in a Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

How do I set a sleep timer?

Open the sleep timer from the player, choose a duration, and decide whether playback stops immediately or after the current track finishes.

How do I edit song information or cover art?

Long-press a song and choose Edit Info to change the title, artist, album, or cover art. Song Info shows the file details stored in your library.

How do lyrics and cover matching work?

Groovy reads lyrics stored with your files and displays them in the lyrics view. You can also add or edit lyrics manually from the lyrics menu. With Pro, Groovy can search for lyrics through the LRCLIB service and for cover art through MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive. Only the song title and artist name are sent, and only when you start a match.

What is Driving Mode?

Driving Mode opens from the full-screen player and shows large, high-contrast playback controls for use while driving.

Why does a song show as missing?

If a file is moved, renamed, or deleted outside the App, Groovy can no longer play it and marks the song as missing. Re-import the file from the Import screen to restore access.

Where is my data stored?

Your library, playlists, lyrics, cover art, and playback state are stored on your device. Groovy does not use an account or cloud sync, and your audio files are not uploaded to our servers. If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, the App's data may be included in that backup according to iOS behavior. Deleting the App removes its library and imported files from the device.

Where can I find the App version?

The version is shown at the bottom of the Settings screen. Please include it when you contact us.
