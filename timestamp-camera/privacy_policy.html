Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 14, 2026

Thank you for using Timestamp Camera (the "App"), provided by the developer reachable at yvente@163.com ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you capture photos and videos, add watermarks, import photos, and save or share your creations.

The current version does not require an account. Photo and video processing, watermark rendering, and AI recommendations take place on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your media. Optional address, map, and weather features use Apple services as described below.

1. Information the App Processes

1.1 Camera, Photos, and Video Audio

  • With your permission, the App accesses the camera to show a preview and capture photos or videos.
  • With microphone permission, the App records audio as part of video recording.
  • When you import a photo through the system photo picker, the App processes the selected photo and available metadata, such as the original date, time zone, and GPS coordinates. The picker does not grant the App unrestricted access to your photo library.
  • Saving a generated photo or video to Apple Photos requires permission to add media to your photo library.
  • Media is processed on your device for cropping, filters, previews, watermark rendering, playback, and export. The App does not upload your photos, videos, or audio to us or to an external AI service.

If a selected photo is stored in iCloud Photos, the system may download it to your device under your Apple settings before it is available to the App.

1.2 Location and Device Sensor Information

When location-dependent watermark elements are enabled and you grant permission, the App uses location information while in use to provide coordinates, addresses, altitude, maps, and weather. This can include latitude, longitude, accuracy, and available altitude readings. Direction information uses the device's heading sensor where supported. The App does not request background location tracking.

You can deny location access or turn off Precise Location in system settings. Some features will then be unavailable or use an approximate location. Imported photos may already contain GPS metadata; denying live location permission does not remove that metadata from a photo you choose to import.

1.3 Watermark Content and Local Preferences

The App processes dates, time zones, notes, and other text you enter, including names, projects, places, or template-specific fields, to create watermarks. Your saved templates, favorites, recent template choices, and camera and watermark preferences are stored on your device. Please avoid including information you do not want others to see in exported media.

1.4 On-Device AI Recommendations

When you request AI recommendations, the App analyzes a camera image or a photo you select on your device using Apple's Vision framework and local matching rules. It uses scene and image characteristics to suggest watermark templates and appearance settings. Images and analysis results are not sent to an external AI provider, and we do not use them to train a server-based model. This feature does not identify individuals.

1.5 Support Communications and Diagnostics

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or diagnostic details you choose to provide. We use them to answer your request and investigate the issue. Please do not send sensitive media unless it is necessary for your request.

The current version does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Apple may process or make available app diagnostics according to your device's analytics settings and Apple's policies; this is separate from the App's local media processing.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To capture, import, edit, preview, and export your media
  • To display the time, location, weather, maps, and text you enable in a watermark
  • To provide on-device template recommendations
  • To maintain your local works library, search saved records, and remember preferences and templates
  • To save media to Apple Photos or share it with a destination you choose
  • To respond to support and privacy inquiries

3. Online Services and Information Sharing

3.1 Apple Location, Maps, and Weather Services

When enabled, address lookup sends location coordinates to Apple's geocoding service to obtain an address. Map watermarks request map data for the relevant location through Apple MapKit. Weather watermarks send the relevant location to Apple WeatherKit to obtain weather data. For imported photos, the relevant location may come from the photo's metadata rather than your current location. The App also downloads weather attribution resources when needed, including when you open the weather data source page.

These requests may expose the requested location and ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to the service provider. They do not include your media or custom watermark text. Apple handles its services under its own policies. See Apple's Privacy Policy and Apple Maps & Privacy. Weather source information is available in the App and on Apple's weather data source page.

3.2 Saving and Sharing Media

When you choose to save or share, the App supplies the generated media to Apple Photos or to the app, service, or recipient you select through the system share sheet. That destination handles the media under its own settings and policies, including any cloud syncing it offers.

Watermarks are embedded in the exported image or video. Anyone who receives the file can see the enabled date, location, address, map, weather, and custom text. Turning off a field or revoking permission later does not remove it from files already created or shared.

3.3 Other Disclosures

We do not sell or rent your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information you send us for support may be handled by our email provider to deliver and store the correspondence, or disclosed when required by applicable law. We cannot retrieve your on-device works library through a developer account or server.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • Generated photos and videos, thumbnails, and associated work records are stored in the App's local storage. Records may include watermark text, time and time zone, enabled location details, and weather information.
  • Templates and preferences are stored locally. Temporary capture and export files and service caches may also be created during use.
  • Saved works remain until you delete them or remove the App's data. Deleting a work removes its record from the App's library and attempts to remove its local files; file cleanup is not a secure-erasure guarantee.
  • Deleting a work does not delete the original imported photo, copies saved in Apple Photos, files shared elsewhere, or copies in device backups.
  • Deleting the App removes its local app data from the device. Offloading the App may retain that data. Depending on your Apple settings, app data may be included in device backups and media saved to Photos may sync through iCloud Photos. The App does not provide its own cloud sync service.
  • Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably needed to address your request, maintain relevant support records, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion, subject to those obligations.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

  • Manage camera, microphone, Photos, and location permissions in system settings
  • Disable optional watermark elements where the selected template supports them, or choose a template without those elements
  • Review watermark text and location information before saving or sharing
  • Delete saved works and custom templates in the App, and manage exported copies and backups separately
  • Delete the App to remove its local data, and manage Apple backup and iCloud settings separately
  • Contact us about information you have provided to us, including requests for access, correction, or deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law, including objection, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent where consent applies, and a right to complain to your local data protection authority. We will respond to applicable requests concerning information we hold. Local media and templates are controlled through your device; we do not hold a server copy.

6. Data Security

The App uses iOS app storage protections and system permission controls. Network requests for attribution resources use HTTPS, and location, map, and weather requests use Apple's system frameworks. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Protect your device and review the content and destination before sharing sensitive media.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose camera and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or below the applicable local age threshold, through support communications. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the App or applicable requirements. The revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated date. For material changes, we will provide additional notice where required and seek consent when applicable law requires it.
