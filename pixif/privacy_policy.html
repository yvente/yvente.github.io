Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 23, 2026

Pixif (the “App”), provided by the developer reachable at yvente@163.com (“we”, “us”, or “our”), is an on-device viewer and editor for photo and video metadata. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you browse your photo library, view or edit EXIF and related metadata, use the map and statistics features, use the Pixif extensions, and manage a Pixif Pro subscription.

Pixif does not require an account and does not operate a server that receives your photos, videos, or metadata. Photo and metadata processing takes place on your device. The App relies on Apple system services for photos, iCloud, maps, and purchases, and on RevenueCat for subscription management. The current version does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. The current version requests photo library and location access; it does not request camera or microphone access.

1. Information the App Processes

1.1 Photo and Video Library

  • With your permission, Pixif accesses your photo library to display photos and videos, read their metadata, analyze them for statistics and maps, and carry out edits, copies, or deletions you request. The App requests read-write access because some features save changes or copies to the library.
  • If you grant limited access, Pixif can only access the items you allow under that setting. You can change photo access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Metadata that Pixif reads and displays may include EXIF fields (such as camera, lens, exposure settings, and capture date), location (GPS) data, IPTC fields (such as caption, creator, copyright, keywords, and rating), and file details (such as format, dimensions, and size). Video items may include creation date, location, and related technical details.
  • Pixif processes this information on your device. We do not upload your photos, videos, or metadata to our servers.
  • If a photo or video is stored in iCloud Photos and the original is not on your device, the system may download it when you open it, edit it, or analyze your library. This depends on your Photos and iCloud settings and on the App’s “Auto Download iCloud Content” preference. These downloads are performed by Apple’s system frameworks.
  • When you save an edit, Pixif asks the system photo library to either create a new copy or replace the selected item, according to the option you choose. Changes may sync through iCloud Photos or other Apple services under your Apple settings.
  • If you choose a delete action, Pixif asks the system to delete the selected photo or video from your library. Apple Photos may retain it in Recently Deleted or elsewhere under its own behavior. Pixif does not keep a separate copy of your library.

1.2 Metadata You View, Copy, Export, or Share

Pixif shows metadata in the App and lets you copy it to the clipboard or export it as a text or CSV file. These actions occur only when you choose them.

When you share an item, Pixif passes the content you select to the system share sheet or another destination you choose. “Share with EXIF” includes the item’s existing metadata, which may include location, capture time, device details, and authorship fields. “Share without EXIF” creates a temporary stripped copy and shares that copy instead; the library item is not changed. Cleaning removes selected metadata categories, but the result may retain technical information needed to display the image and other embedded metadata. Cleaning is not a guarantee that all identifying information has been removed. Review the file and the destination before sharing.

The Action Extension works on media you send to it from another app. It copies the selected items into a temporary workspace, can show metadata for a single image, and can share the originals or create cleaned copies. This processing happens on your device. Temporary files are removed when the extension finishes or is cancelled, and the original items are not modified.

The photo editing extension displays metadata for an item you open in Apple Photos. It does not save changes to that item.

1.3 Location

  • With your permission, Pixif uses your location while you are using the App to show your position on the map or to help set a location for a photo or video. Pixif does not request background location access.
  • When you search for a place or convert a coordinate into an address, the search text or coordinate is sent to Apple’s map and geocoding services to return results. Those requests are handled by Apple and are subject to Apple’s policies; we do not receive them.
  • Photos and videos may contain location metadata from your camera or from edits you make. You can edit or remove this metadata, and you can choose whether to include metadata when sharing. Denying location access does not remove location metadata already stored in your library.

1.4 Statistics and Local Index

When you run the statistics feature, Pixif reads metadata from the photos and videos in the scope you choose and builds an index on your device. The index may contain asset identifiers, capture and library dates, camera and lens names, focal length, ISO, aperture, shutter speed, file format, a screenshot flag, and coarse location bins (rounded to a grid) used for the footprint view. It does not contain copies of your photos or videos. The statistics report focuses on photos.

The index is stored in the App’s local storage and is not uploaded to us or synced through a developer-operated service. It is updated when the system reports library changes and remains until you delete the App or its data. Statistics reports are generated on your device. If you share a report, the generated image is sent only to the destination you choose; a report can include an optional representative photo you select and, if you enable it, an approximate location map.

1.5 Pixif Pro Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to display subscription options, determine entitlement status, and support restoring purchases. RevenueCat may process an automatically generated anonymous App User ID, product and entitlement identifiers, subscription status, transaction or receipt information, last-seen time, limited device and app information, and ordinary connection information such as an IP address. We receive subscription status and related purchase records, but we do not receive your full payment card details. For more information, see RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

1.6 Support Communications and Preferences

If you contact support from the App, Pixif prepares an email draft addressed to yvente@163.com that includes the App version, your device model, your iOS version, and the message you write. No photos, videos, or metadata are attached automatically. The draft is sent only if you choose to send it, and we receive the information you include.

Your appearance choice, explanation setting, card visibility, onboarding state, and iCloud auto-download preference are stored locally on your device.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To display photos, videos, thumbnails, and metadata
  • To edit, save, clean, copy, export, or delete items at your request
  • To build local statistics, comparisons, and map views
  • To share content with a destination you select
  • To load subscription options, verify entitlement, and restore purchases
  • To respond to support and privacy inquiries

We do not use your photos, videos, or metadata for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing and Third-Party Services

3.1 Apple Services

Pixif relies on Apple frameworks and services for access to Photos and iCloud Photos, map display and map search, geocoding, and App Store purchases. Apple may process the relevant requests under its own policies. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

3.2 RevenueCat

RevenueCat processes the subscription-related information described above to provide entitlement and purchase-management services. Its handling is governed by its own privacy policy.

3.3 Destinations You Choose

When you share, export, copy, or email content, the destination you choose handles it under its own terms and privacy practices. This includes files you share containing metadata, cleaned copies, and report images. We cannot control or retrieve content after you share it.

3.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information we hold, such as support correspondence, if required by applicable law or necessary to respond to lawful requests. We cannot disclose your local photo library or statistics index because we do not have access to them.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • Photos, videos, edits, and copies are stored in your photo library on your device and, if enabled, in iCloud Photos. We do not store them on our own servers.
  • The statistics index and App preferences are stored locally. Deleting the App removes its local data from the device; offloading the App may retain data, and device backups may include it depending on your Apple settings.
  • Temporary files created by the extensions are kept in the App’s temporary workspace and are removed when the extension finishes or is cancelled, subject to iOS cleanup.
  • Subscription information is retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their policies and legal requirements. Support correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably needed to address the request and meet applicable obligations.
  • Deleting the App does not delete photos or videos from your library, copies saved elsewhere, or files you shared. Deleting an item from the App affects the library item you selected, subject to Apple’s Recently Deleted behavior.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

  • Manage Photos and Location permissions in iOS Settings, including limited photo access
  • Review metadata before sharing, choose “Share with EXIF” or “Share without EXIF,” and create cleaned copies
  • Edit or remove metadata, and delete photos or videos from your library when you choose
  • Disable automatic iCloud downloads in the App
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings, and use Restore Purchases in the App
  • Delete the App to remove its local data, and manage backups and iCloud Photos separately
  • Contact us about information you have provided to us, including access, correction, or deletion requests

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law. We will respond to applicable requests concerning information we hold, such as support correspondence and subscription records. Local photos, metadata, and the statistics index are controlled through your device, and we do not hold a server copy.

6. Data Security

Pixif processes photos and metadata on your device and relies on iOS app storage protections and permission controls. Network requests made through Apple services and RevenueCat use system frameworks and HTTPS. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Metadata can reveal sensitive information such as where and when a photo was taken, so review items and files before sharing them.

7. Children’s Privacy

Pixif is not directed to children under 13, or below the applicable age threshold where you live. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through support communications. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can address it.
