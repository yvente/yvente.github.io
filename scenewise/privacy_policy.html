Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SceneWise (the "App") handles the videos and related information that you import, play and organize with the App.

In short: SceneWise is a local video player. Your videos, playback progress and playback statistics are stored and processed entirely on your device. The developer never uploads your videos or any personal information to a server. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require account registration and the developer does not collect personal information for advertising, tracking or analytics purposes. The developer does not operate any server that receives your media or usage data.

1.1 Videos you import

Videos enter the App only through actions you take:

  • Selecting videos from your photo library through the system photo picker. The App accesses only the items you select.
  • Selecting videos from the Files app. File-backed items are accessed using a security-scoped reference for the duration of the import task only.
  • Receiving videos through the App's Wi-Fi transfer feature, which is described in Section 3.
  • Copying videos into the App's folder from a computer using Finder or iTunes.

Imported videos are copied into the App's private storage on your device. If a selected photo-library item is stored in iCloud and is not currently available on your device, Apple's Photos framework may download it from iCloud so the App can import it locally. This transfer is handled by Apple under your iCloud settings and Apple's privacy terms; the App does not upload the item to any developer server.

1.2 Technical information generated during import

To build your library, the App reads and generates on your device:

  • Video file names and durations
  • Thumbnail images generated from your videos
  • The titles you set when renaming a video

This information stays in the App's storage on your device and is used only to display and organize your library.

1.3 Playback information stored on your device

To provide resume playback and the statistics screen, the App stores the following on your device only:

  • Playback progress so you can resume where you left off
  • Playback statistics such as watch time and play counts
  • App preferences such as playback speed

This information never leaves your device and is never associated with your identity by the developer.

1.4 Face ID

If you enable library protection, the App asks iOS to verify your identity with Face ID before showing your library. Face ID data is processed by Apple's system secure enclave; the App never sees or stores your biometric data.

2. How the App Uses Information

Imported videos and the information generated on-device are used only to provide the following features:

  • Displaying your private video library with thumbnails and search
  • Playing videos with resume progress, seeking, skip, playback speed and Picture in Picture
  • Renaming and deleting videos in your library
  • Showing playback statistics
  • Receiving videos you send from a browser on your computer over your local network

3. Wi-Fi Transfer on Your Local Network

The App includes a Wi-Fi transfer feature that lets you import videos from a browser on a computer connected to the same local network. When you turn the feature on:

  • The App starts a temporary local server on your device and advertises it on your local network.
  • Videos you send from the browser are transferred directly to your device. They are not routed through, stored on or visible to any server operated by the developer or any third party.
  • Access is protected by a username and a random password generated for each session, and the server stops when you turn the feature off or close the App.

Please use this feature only on networks you trust, because any device on the same local network could attempt to reach the transfer page while it is running.

4. Third-Party Sharing and External Transmission

The App does not transmit your videos, playback information or personal information to any server operated by the developer or third parties, and does not sell or share them with anyone.

The App does not include a feature to share or export your videos. The only share option in the App (Settings, "Share SceneWise") shares the App Store link only and does not include any of your media.

5. Data Storage and Retention

  • All videos and playback information are stored in the App's private storage on your device.
  • Deleting a video inside the App removes it and its thumbnail from the App's storage.
  • The developer's servers never store your media or playback information, because the developer operates no such servers.
  • Deleting the App removes the library and all data kept in the App's storage from your device. Your original photos and files outside the App are not modified or deleted by the App.

6. External Links

The App may contain links to external pages for support, privacy policy, terms of use or similar purposes. Once you open an external site, the policy of that site applies to how information is handled there.

7. Your Controls

You can take the following actions at any time:

  • Delete any video from the library inside the App
  • Clear playback statistics from within the App
  • Turn off or keep off the Wi-Fi transfer feature, and change the local network permission in iOS Settings
  • Change Face ID protection in the App's settings, and Face ID enrollment in iOS Settings
  • Delete the App to remove all data held within the App's storage
  • Contact the developer with privacy questions

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general use. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App, and the App does not transmit personal information anywhere.

9. Security

By keeping all video storage and playback on-device, the App minimizes any opportunity for your media to be transmitted externally. The Wi-Fi transfer feature is protected by a per-session password and is active only while you are using it. However, no device, operating system or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please use appropriate safeguards such as a device passcode and keeping the operating system up to date.

10. Changes to This Policy

The developer may update this policy as the App's features, the technologies it uses, or legal requirements change. When important changes occur, they will be announced on this page or within the App. The update date is shown at the top of this page.
