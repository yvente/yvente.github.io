Lumo Translate Support

Last Updated: October 30, 2026

Lumo Translate is a translation app for iPhone that uses Apple's on-device translation. It covers text translation, two-way voice conversations, camera and photo translation, document translation, a translation keyboard, a share extension, Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and an Apple Watch companion. This page answers common questions and explains how to reach us.

Contact Us

For questions, bug reports, or feature requests, email us at yvente@163.com.

To help us investigate quickly, please include:

  • Your device model (for example, iPhone 17 Pro) and iOS version
  • The App version and the language pair you were using
  • The steps that led to the problem and the exact message you saw

The App's support option opens an email draft that includes the App and iOS version automatically. Please do not attach documents, photos, or recordings unless you choose to share them for troubleshooting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the App free?

Yes. Basic text translation, photo translation (camera capture, photo library import, and selected image regions), manually started two-way conversations, history, favorites, wordbooks, widgets, and the Apple Watch companion are available without a subscription. There are no ads and no daily usage limits.

What does Pro include?

  • Live camera translation with continuous on-screen results
  • The translation keyboard: translate typed, selected, or pasted text (basic typing stays free)
  • Share extension translation for text, webpages, and images from other apps (reading shared text and local text recognition stay free)
  • Document translation for PDF, TXT, Markdown, HTML, and Word (.docx) files
  • Scanned document translation for multi-page paper documents
  • Hands-free continuous conversations that listen, translate, and speak back and forth

Pro is an auto-renewable subscription. Starting a document translation or a multi-page scan, or resuming or retranslating an unfinished document, requires active Pro access. Documents you have already translated remain available for viewing, copying, and export after Pro access ends. Pro does not remove file-size, page, character, device, or language limitations.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed through your Apple Account and renew automatically unless cancelled in Apple's subscription settings. You can restore a purchase in the App under More → Settings → Restore Purchases with the Apple Account you used to buy it. Deleting the App or its local data does not cancel a subscription.

Which languages are supported?

Translation uses the languages provided by iOS on your device, so availability depends on your device model, iOS version, and language pair. Not every feature supports every language.

How does offline translation work?

Open More → Offline readiness to check the selected language pair and prepare the language resources iOS needs. Prepare supported resources before travelling; once prepared, translation runs on your device without a network connection. Device and language availability vary.

How do I set up the translation keyboard?

Add it under iOS Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard, then enable Allow Full Access for translation features. Basic typing works without Full Access or a subscription; translating typed, selected, or pasted text requires Pro. You always preview and confirm a translation before it replaces text.

How does share to translate work?

Share text, a webpage, or an image from another app to translate it. You can read the shared text, copy the original, and see locally recognized text in images for free; submitting the translation requires Pro.

Does the App work with Apple Watch?

Yes. Open More → Apple Watch on your iPhone to choose what syncs to your paired watch, such as wordbooks, saved words and phrases, and daily vocabulary. You can view saved content and hear it spoken on the watch, including offline. All Apple Watch features are free.

Where is my data stored?

History, conversations, drafts, favorites, wordbooks, and imported documents are stored locally on your device. The App has no account and no cloud sync. Purchases are managed by Apple and RevenueCat using an anonymous identifier; your translation text, images, documents, and recordings are not sent to RevenueCat. Watch content transfers directly between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's Watch Connectivity framework. Deleting the App removes its local data; it does not cancel a subscription.

Which permissions does the App use?

Camera, microphone, and speech recognition are requested only when you use the related features, and you can change them anytime in iOS Settings. Basic text translation does not require camera or microphone access.

Why couldn't the keyboard replace my text in some apps?

Some apps restrict how keyboards may change their content. When replacement is not possible, your original text is kept in the keyboard's preview so you can copy the translation instead.

Where can I find the App version?

The support email draft from the App includes the App and iOS version automatically. If you email us directly, please copy the version from the App Store page or include it in your message.
