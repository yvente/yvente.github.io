Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumo (the “App”) handles information when you use its translation, camera, voice, document, and keyboard features. Lumo is designed to process your content on your device. Please read this Policy before using the App.

1. Information We Process

1.1 Text Translations

When you request a translation, the text you enter or select and the resulting translation are processed using Apple’s system translation services on your device for supported language pairs. The App does not send translation text to a Lumo-operated server. Apple may require a network connection to download language resources before they are ready; those resources are managed by the operating system.

Text translations may be saved in the App’s local history. The history keeps up to 200 recent entries. If you choose to save a word or phrase, that item is stored in your local wordbook or favorites.

1.2 Voice Conversations

If you choose to start voice translation, the App requests microphone and Speech Recognition permissions. Voice recognition starts only when the device reports that on-device recognition is available. The App requires on-device recognition and will not start recording for a language or device that cannot provide it.

  • Audio is used temporarily to recognize the words you speak and is not saved as an audio recording by Lumo.
  • Your recognized text and translation appear in the current conversation. The conversation is not automatically added to translation history.
  • If you choose to save a conversation translation as a word or favorite, that text is stored locally.
  • Spoken playback uses Apple’s built-in speech synthesis on your device.

1.3 Camera, Photos, and Scanned Documents

The camera feature uses the camera only while you are using the camera screen. You may also choose an image with Apple’s photo picker. Lumo uses Apple’s on-device text recognition and translation frameworks to analyze the captured or selected image. Lumo does not upload these images to its servers.

A translated photo remains in the active screen unless you choose to share it. If you scan pages and choose “Save and translate,” the resulting scanned document and its translation are kept in the App’s local document library.

1.4 Imported Documents

When you choose a PDF, TXT, Markdown, HTML, or DOCX file to translate, the App copies that file into its local storage and saves extracted text, translation progress, and results there. Scanned PDFs may be processed with on-device text recognition. Lumo does not upload your imported document or its translated text to a Lumo-operated server.

You can delete an imported document from the App’s library. This deletes the App’s copy and saved translation; it does not delete the original file in its original location. Files you export or share are handled by the destination you select.

1.5 Translation Keyboard

The keyboard uses text that you type with Lumo or explicitly select for translation. It does not automatically read or translate an entire message or conversation. The Paste button reads clipboard text only after you tap it and grant the access required by iOS. Translation results are shown for your review; Lumo does not send or submit messages on your behalf.

1.6 Preferences and Widgets

Language choices and other preferences are stored on your device. The Lumo widget may read the selected translation languages from the shared app group so it can open the relevant feature. Translation text, conversation content, and document contents are not shared with the widget.

1.7 Support Messages

If you contact support from the App, Lumo prepares an email draft addressed to yvente@163.com with the App and iOS version. Your translations, documents, and recordings are not attached automatically. The draft is sent only if you choose to send it, and any information you include is handled by your email provider and the recipient.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the App is used to provide the feature you request: translate text, recognize speech, recognize text in images or documents, display your local history and saved phrases, and remember your settings. Lumo does not currently use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not operate an account, advertising, or translation server.

3. Sharing and System Services

Lumo does not sell your personal information or share your translation content with third parties through a Lumo server. The App relies on Apple system frameworks for translation, speech recognition, speech synthesis, camera access, and text recognition. Apple’s operating system may download language resources or process diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple’s own terms and privacy practices. When you use the system share sheet or send a support email, the content you choose to share is handled by the service or person you select.

4. Storage and Retention

  • Translation history, saved words and phrases, preferences, imported documents, and document translations are stored in the App’s local storage.
  • Voice audio is not retained by Lumo after recognition. An unsaved voice conversation remains only in the active conversation view.
  • You can delete individual history entries, saved phrases, and documents, or use “Delete all local data” in Settings to remove the App’s saved content and reset preferences.
  • Device backups may include App data according to your iOS backup settings. Deleting data in Lumo does not remove files you exported or shared, Apple-managed language resources, or copies in backups.
  • Deleting the App removes its local app data according to iOS behavior. Data held by Apple, your email provider, or a destination you chose is governed by that provider’s retention practices.

5. Permissions and Your Choices

Camera, microphone, and Speech Recognition access are requested only for the corresponding features. You can change or revoke these permissions in iOS Settings. You can use text translation without granting camera or microphone access. You may review or delete locally saved content in the App at any time.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. Lumo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support email, please contact us so we can address the request.

7. Security

Lumo stores saved content within the App’s protected storage on your device and does not maintain a Lumo cloud database. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Please use the device security features available to you and avoid entering information into any app unless you are comfortable processing it for the requested feature.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or its data practices change. The “Last Updated” date at the top indicates when this Policy was most recently revised. Continued use of the App after an update means the revised Policy applies to subsequent use.
