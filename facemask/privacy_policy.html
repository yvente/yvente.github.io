FaceMask Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FaceMask (the “App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use the App. It applies to the App and related support communications.

Privacy at a glance
  • Photos, face detection, masking, stickers, and manual edits are processed on your device.
  • We do not upload your photos, face regions, or editing content to our servers.
  • If you save an Artwork, the rendered image and related metadata remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • Firebase processes limited usage and diagnostic data, and RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription data.

1. What FaceMask Does

FaceMask is a face-privacy editing tool. It can detect faces automatically and lets you apply generated privacy covers (including rainbow grid, black-and-white privacy grid, and solid color), blur or pixelation effects, stickers, and manual adjustments. You can review the result, save it as an in-app Artwork, export it to Photos, or share it using the iOS share sheet.

2. Photos and Face Processing

2.1 Photos and camera images

The App receives a photo only when you select it, open an Artwork already stored by the App, or capture a photo with the camera. Your original and edited images are processed locally on your device. They are not sent to Firebase, RevenueCat, or our servers for face detection or image editing.

2.2 Face detection data

The App uses Apple’s Vision framework on your device to identify face regions. During an editing session, it may temporarily use face bounding boxes and detection confidence values to position effects. The App does not use this information to identify a person, compare identities, authenticate users, or create a biometric profile or faceprint.

Temporary face-detection results are used for the current editing workflow and are not uploaded to us. Closing or leaving the workflow releases temporary editing data in accordance with normal iOS memory management.

2.3 Content you choose to save

The App does not automatically store every edited photo. When you choose to save an Artwork, however, the App stores the rendered image, a thumbnail, creation date, title, effect description, and detected face count in the App’s local storage. When you export or share an image, Apple’s Photos service or the destination you choose may retain a copy under its own terms.

Camera photos may also be saved to Photos when the App’s auto-save setting is enabled. You can change this preference in the App’s settings.

3. Device Permissions

Depending on the feature you use, the App may request:

  • Photos access: to let you select images and save processed results.
  • Camera access: to capture a photo for editing.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings. If you deny a permission, the related feature may be unavailable, but other parts of the App may continue to work.

4. Other Information We Process

4.1 Usage and diagnostics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data, including:

  • Number of App launches
  • Frequency of feature usage
  • Crash reports

This data is completely anonymous and does not contain any personally identifiable information.

4.2 Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to display offerings and determine subscription or purchase entitlement status. RevenueCat may process an automatically generated anonymous App User ID, product and entitlement identifiers, subscription status, transaction or receipt information, last-seen time, and limited device or app information. We do not receive your full payment-card details. For more information, see RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

4.3 Local preferences

The App stores settings such as haptic feedback, automatic photo saving, review-prompt timing, and similar preferences locally on your device.

4.4 Communications

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. We use it to respond to your request and provide support.

5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide face detection, privacy effects, stickers, manual editing, saving, exporting, and sharing;
  • Remember your settings and locally saved Artworks;
  • Verify purchases, restore entitlements, and manage access to premium features;
  • Measure feature performance, troubleshoot errors, prevent abuse, and improve the App;
  • Respond to support and privacy requests; and
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your photos or face-detection regions with Firebase or RevenueCat. We may disclose limited information:

  • To Google Firebase for analytics and crash diagnostics;
  • To RevenueCat for purchase and entitlement management;
  • To Apple when you use Photos, the camera, sharing, App Store purchases, subscription management, or other Apple services;
  • When required by law, legal process, or to protect users, our rights, or the security of the App; or
  • In connection with a business transfer, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

If you choose to share an edited image, the recipient or third-party destination you select will handle that image under its own privacy practices.

7. Storage and Retention

  • Artworks and preferences: retained locally until you delete the Artwork, clear applicable App data, or uninstall the App.
  • Photos exports: retained in Photos or by a sharing destination until you remove them there.
  • Firebase and RevenueCat data: retained according to their policies and operational or legal requirements.
  • Support emails: retained as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations.

Deleting the App removes its local container from your device under normal iOS behavior, but it does not automatically delete copies exported to Photos, shared elsewhere, Apple purchase records, or information retained by service providers under their policies.

8. International Processing

Firebase and RevenueCat may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Where required, they and we rely on appropriate legal mechanisms and safeguards for international transfers.

9. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

  • Review, delete, or export locally saved Artworks in the App;
  • Change camera and Photos permissions in iOS Settings;
  • Change automatic saving and haptic preferences in the App;
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID subscription settings;
  • Delete the App to remove locally stored App data; and
  • Contact us to exercise privacy rights available in your jurisdiction.

We may need reasonable information to verify and process a request. Some records may be controlled by Apple or held by a service provider, and some information may be retained where required by law.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No device, storage method, or transmission system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review the request.

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, service providers, or legal requirements. We will update the effective date above and provide additional notice when required by law.
