Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 16, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use StandBy Clock (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). This Privacy Policy is intended to inform you about how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Widget Configuration Data

To provide widget display functionality, the App stores your widget configurations locally on your device, including:

  • Widget style preferences (clock, digital clock, calendar, dual time zone, single time zone, countdown, info card, battery, GIF, photo)
  • Theme customization settings (colors, fonts, layout options)
  • Countdown event names and target dates
  • Info card display content

1.2 Photo Library Access

To enable photo and GIF widget features, we require access to your photo library. We only access photos when you actively select them, and:

  • Selected photos are resized and stored locally on your device for widget display
  • We do not upload your photos to any server
  • You can revoke photo access permission at any time through system settings

1.3 App Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage data to improve the App, including:

  • Number of App launches
  • Frequency of feature usage
  • Crash reports

This data is completely anonymous and does not contain any personally identifiable information.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To store and display your configured widgets on the iOS StandBy screen
  • To refresh widget content at appropriate intervals (clock times, calendar dates, countdown progress, battery levels)
  • To improve App performance and user experience
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues

3. Information Sharing

We do not share your photos, widget configurations, or any personal data with any third parties. All widget data is stored and processed locally on your device.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • All widget configurations are stored locally on your device
  • Selected photos and GIFs are stored locally in the App's shared container
  • We do not store any personal data on our servers
  • Uninstalling the App will permanently delete all locally stored data

5. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Revoke photo library access permission through system settings
  • Delete any widget to remove its configuration data
  • Delete the App to clear all related data
  • Contact us with privacy-related questions

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent.

7. Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be announced within the App or on our website.
