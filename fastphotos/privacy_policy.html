Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 15, 2026

Thank you for choosing to use FastPhotos (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). This Privacy Policy is intended to inform you about how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Photo Library Access

To enable the photo album management and browsing features, we require access to your photo library. We only access photos when you actively use the App, and:

  • Photos are analyzed locally on your device
  • We do not upload your photos to any server
  • You can revoke photo access permission at any time through system settings

1.2 Location Access

To display photo location information on the map, we may request access to your location. We:

  • Only use location data to show where your photos were taken on the map
  • Do not upload your location data to any server
  • All location processing is performed locally on your device
  • You can revoke location access permission at any time through system settings

1.3 Photo Metadata

When processing photos, the App may access photo metadata including:

  • Photo creation date and size
  • Image file format
  • Camera and lens information
  • Location information (used for map display)

1.4 App Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage data to improve the App, including:

  • Number of App launches
  • Frequency of feature usage
  • Crash reports and performance data

This data is completely anonymous and does not contain any personally identifiable information.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To browse, organize, and manage your photo and video albums
  • To detect and group similar photos
  • To display photo EXIF information
  • To show photo locations on the map
  • To search and filter photos by various criteria
  • To improve App performance and user experience
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues

3. Information Sharing

We do not share your photos or any personal data with any third parties. All processing is performed locally on your device.

4. Data Storage and Retention

  • All photo analysis is conducted on your device
  • We do not store any photos on our servers
  • All temporary data is automatically cleared when the App is closed
  • Photo tags and organizational data created by you are stored locally using on-device data storage

5. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Revoke photo and location access permissions through system settings
  • Delete the App to clear all related data
  • Contact us with privacy-related questions

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 without parental consent.

7. Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be announced within the App or on our website.
