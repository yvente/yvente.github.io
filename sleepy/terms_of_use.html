5. App Description and Functionality

5.1 Sleep Reminders

The App provides bedtime reminder notifications on your Apple Watch. You configure a desired bedtime and a nagging interval, and the App sends repeated local notifications until you confirm that you are going to sleep.

5.2 Sleep History

The App records your bedtime confirmation times and calculates a "struggle score" (how long you stayed up after the first reminder). This history is stored locally on your device and displayed in a statistics view.

5.3 Local Notifications Only

All reminders are delivered as local notifications. The App does not use push notifications or require an internet connection to function.

5.4 No Health Data

The App does not access HealthKit, sleep tracking data, heart rate, or any other health-related information. Sleep data is based solely on your self-reported bedtime confirmations.
